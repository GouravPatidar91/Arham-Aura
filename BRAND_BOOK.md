## 2. Brand Archetype & Tone of Voice

### Brand Archetype
* **The Creator & The Romantic**: Sensual, aesthetic, meticulous craftsmanship, warm sanctuary, and heartfelt gifting.

### Tone of Voice
* **Warm & Intimate**: Welcoming each customer into an artisan sanctuary (*"Hand-poured with love"*).
* **Poetic & Sensory**: Evoking sensory bliss—scents of calm, warmth of flickering flames, and the delicate beauty of blooming florals.
* **Refined & Luxurious**: Quiet luxury, sophisticated composure, grounded in warm natural textures and delicate pastels.

## 3. Logo Anatomy & Visual Motifs

1. **The Royal Archway / Jharokha (झरोखा)**:
   - Symbolizes timeless royal Indian heritage, serene sanctuary, and architectural elegance.
2. **The Sacred Lotus (कमल)**:
   - Positioned at the pinnacle of the arch; represents purity, divine serenity, and blooming life.
3. **The Celestial Crescent & Foliage**:
   - Represents the nocturnal glow of candlelight, peaceful aura, and botanical essence.
4. **Bilingual Brand Signature**:
   - Devanagari script **अर्हम** centered with Latin serif **AURA** below.

## 5. Typography System

### Primary Editorial Serif (Headings & Luxury Statements)
* **Fonts**: `Cormorant Garamond` / `Playfair Display`
* **Styling**: High-contrast classic serif, tracked letter spacing for all-caps (`letter-spacing: 0.15em`).

### Secondary Sans-Serif (Body, Specs & UI)
* **Fonts**: `Montserrat` / `Jost` / `Inter`
* **Styling**: Geometric, highly legible, airy line-height (`line-height: 1.6`).

### Accent Calligraphic Script (Sub-headings & Romantic Details)
* **Fonts**: `Great Vibes` / `Pinyon Script`
* **Styling**: Flowing handwriting for intimate phrases like *"Made with love"*, *"Daisy collection"*.

### Devanagari Typography
* **Fonts**: `Rozha One` / `Noto Serif Devanagari`
* **Styling**: Traditional elegance for the brand's cultural identity.
